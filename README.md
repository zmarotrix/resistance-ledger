# The Resistance Ledger

An interactive, sourced explainer on spell resistance in Classic World of Warcraft (patch 1.12 rules), plus what WoW Forever has confirmed so far.

**Read it:** https://zmarotrix.github.io/resistance-ledger/

What's inside:

- How the two spell rolls work (hit, then resistance), with a scroll-driven diagram
- Charts for miss chance, average resistance and partial-resist slices
- A simulator that casts thousands of spells and checks the result against the formula
- Raid-by-raid requirements for Molten Core, Onyxia, Blackwing Lair, AQ40 and Naxxramas, and how guilds skip them
- A resistance stack calculator
- WoW Forever: what Blizzard confirmed, what guides report, and our forecast, kept separate
- Common misconceptions, each with a verdict and a source
- A plain-English glossary, and 38 numbered sources graded by reliability

Each chapter opens with an "In plain words" summary. The formulas and the points where sources disagree are in expandable "Technical detail" sections.
