# The Resistance Ledger

Why raiders wear magic resistance gear in Classic World of Warcraft (patch 1.12): how resistance works, which bosses need it during progression and which still need it on farm, how guilds skip it, and what WoW Forever has confirmed so far.

**Read it:** https://zmarotrix.github.io/resistance-ledger/

## What's inside

- **Survive it:** pull Ragnaros, Huhuran or Sapphiron with your own resistance and healing, and watch partial resists roll live.
- **How it works:** the hit roll and the resistance roll, with a scroll-driven diagram and interactive charts.
- **Progression vs farm:** a boss-by-boss heat map of when resistance is needed and when it stops being needed.
- **The raids:** Molten Core, Onyxia, Blackwing Lair, AQ40 and Naxxramas, with every ability's icon and numbers.
- **Stack calculator** and a **simulator** that checks the formulas over up to 100,000 casts.
- **WoW Forever:** what Blizzard confirmed, what guides report, and our read, kept separate.
- **Myth or fact:** 13 common claims, each with a verdict and its sources.
- **[Is VMaNGOS credible?](https://zmarotrix.github.io/resistance-ledger/vmangos.html):** every number was cross-checked against the open-source VMaNGOS server. This page covers what that check is worth.

Each section opens with a 10-second version. The depth is in expandable sections.

## Files

`index.html`, `vmangos.html`, `site.css`, `data.js` (bosses, abilities, myths, evidence) and `app.js` (interactive parts). No build step. The images are loading screens and icons from the 1.12 game client.
